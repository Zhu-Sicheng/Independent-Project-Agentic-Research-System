## overnight_intraday_return_divergence_20d

**hypothesis**: Stocks with high 20-day cumulative overnight return minus 20-day cumulative intraday return underperform stocks with low values over the next 5 trading days.

**economic rationale**: Overnight and intraday returns are driven by different investor clienteles: overnight moves reflect informed/retail attention and news absorption at the open, while intraday moves reflect liquidity provision and institutional flow. When a stock's cumulative overnight return persistently diverges from its cumulative intraday return over a month, the divergence reflects a persistent demand imbalance that market makers are compensated for absorbing. Stocks where overnight returns have run ahead of intraday returns tend to be overbought by attention-driven buyers and subsequently underperform as the imbalance corrects.

## close_position_persistence_20d

**hypothesis**: Stocks with high average close_position over the trailing 20 days underperform stocks with low average close_position over the next 5 trading days.

**economic rationale**: Where a stock closes within its daily high-low range signals whether buyers or sellers won the session. Persistent closing near the top of the range over a month indicates sustained buying pressure that is partly transient (liquidity-demand driven) rather than fundamental, and tends to mean-revert. Conversely, stocks that repeatedly close weak have absorbed selling pressure and are positioned to rebound as that pressure exhausts. The premium is compensation for providing liquidity against persistent one-sided order flow.

## range_to_overnight_gap_ratio_20d
