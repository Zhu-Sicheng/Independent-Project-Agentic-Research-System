## overnight_intraday_divergence

**hypothesis**: Stocks with high cumulative overnight return relative to cumulative intraday return over the past 20 days underperform stocks with the opposite pattern over the next 5 trading days; i.e., the divergence factor has negative IC.

**economic rationale**: Overnight and intraday returns are driven by different clienteles: overnight moves are dominated by retail/after-hours order flow and news gaps, while intraday moves reflect institutional liquidity provision. Stocks whose recent returns came disproportionately from the overnight session tend to be pushed by sentiment-driven retail demand that later mean-reverts during liquid hours, whereas stocks earning returns intraday reflect informed institutional accumulation that persists. The divergence between the two components isolates the noise-vs-information split.

## close_position_persistence

Persistent high close positions signal sustained buying pressure absorbed by liquidity providers who require compensation, and the pressure tends to bleed into subsequent days before reversing. Conversely, stocks consistently closing near their lows reflect persistent selling pressure that continues short-term. This captures short-horizon momentum in the intraday order-flow imbalance that total returns dilute.
