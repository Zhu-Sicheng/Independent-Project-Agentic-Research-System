## close_location_reversal_5d

**hypothesis**: Stocks with the highest average close_position over the past 5 days underperform stocks with the lowest average close_position over the next 5 trading days (negative IC).

**economic rationale**: Where a stock closes within its daily high-low range reflects the balance of buying versus selling pressure within the session. Extreme closes near the high (or low) are often driven by transient order-flow and liquidity demand rather than information, and tend to mean-revert because liquidity providers who absorbed the imbalance are compensated once pressure fades. Because the raw close_position is already a bounded, cross-sectionally comparable [0,1] quantity, ranking it isolates the pressure effect from price level and volatility.

The stability battery shows a window Sharpe span of [0.03, 0.88] and decay span down to 0.36, plus variant_pnl_decorrelates (min_pnl_corr 0.28), so the headline edge is a single-configuration artifact. The close-location reversal mechanism simply does not persist in modern data.
- **diagnosis**: Overfit to the 2000-2020 sample; the intraday-pressure reversal edge has decayed to nothing out of sample and is not identified by variants (window/decay sensitivity, decorrelated variant PnL).

## overnight_gap_absorption_ratio

**hypothesis**: Among stocks with positive cumulative 20-day overnight returns, those whose intraday returns fail to confirm the gap (low intraday/overnight ratio) underperform those with confirming intraday drift over the next 20 trading days (negative IC on the ratio).

**economic rationale**: Overnight returns embed information (news, earnings, macro) that is impounded when the market is closed and illiquid, while intraday returns represent the liquid, informed trading session. When a large overnight gap is not confirmed by the subsequent intraday move (i.e., intraday partially absorbs it), the gap is more likely to reflect uninformed liquidity demand or stale pricing and should reverse. Conversely, gaps confirmed by same-direction intraday drift carry genuine information. The ratio of cumulative intraday to cumulative overnight return over 20 days captures this confirmation/absorption dynamic.

best config: decay=0, transform=rank, cap_filter=0.3, windows=[20, 20], params={}

- **decision**: abandon
- **reasoning**: The full-universe Sharpe of 0.46 is already weak, and the untouched segments confirm decay: OOS Sharpe 0.19 with rank IC of just 0.0011, and holdout Sharpe -0.04. The IS edge is itself unstable (2007, 2015, 2017 strongly negative; 2020/2021 near zero) and the window_sharpe_span crossing zero (-0.14 to 0.86) shows the result is an artifact of the arbitrary 20-day choice. The mechanism is not identified by these data.
- **diagnosis**: Signal is essentially absent: near-zero IC that collapses out of sample, with performance driven by one arbitrary window choice rather than a persistent confirmation/absorption effect.
