## overnight_gap_absorption_ratio

**hypothesis**: Stocks whose 20-day cumulative intraday return is large relative to their cumulative overnight return (gap-confirmation) outperform stocks where intraday flow fades overnight gaps, over the next 5 trading days.

**economic rationale**: Overnight order flow reflects uninformed retail and news-driven liquidity demand that is often reversed during the continuous session as market makers and informed traders absorb the imbalance. When a stock's cumulative overnight moves are systematically NOT reversed intraday (i.e., the intraday session confirms the gap direction), the gap reflects genuine information and tends to continue. The ratio of cumulative intraday return to cumulative overnight return over a short window captures whether the continuous market validates or fades the overnight move.

Stocks with high volume-confirmed overnight drift tend to continue because the volume validates that the move was driven by real demand rather than thin-market noise.
