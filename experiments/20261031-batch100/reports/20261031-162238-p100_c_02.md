## intraday_overnight_return_divergence

**hypothesis**: Stocks whose 20-day cumulative overnight return minus cumulative intraday return is most negative (overnight underperformance relative to intraday) outperform over the next 5 trading days; the long/short spread is positive.

**economic rationale**: Overnight and intraday returns are driven by different clienteles: overnight moves reflect institutional/global information flow and gap risk, while intraday moves reflect retail order flow and market-maker inventory absorption. When a stock's cumulative overnight return persistently diverges from its cumulative intraday return over a month, the divergence reflects a temporary inventory imbalance that market makers are paid to absorb, and it mean-reverts as positioning unwinds. The premium is compensation for bearing the inventory risk of the side that is accumulating the position.
