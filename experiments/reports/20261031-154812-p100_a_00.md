## close_position_trend_persistence

**hypothesis**: Stocks whose average close_position over the past 20 days is persistently high outperform stocks with persistently low average close_position over the next 5 trading days, dollar-neutral.

**economic rationale**: Where a stock closes within its daily high-low range is a noisy but informative proxy for end-of-day order-flow imbalance. Persistent high close positions over a multi-week window indicate sustained accumulation by informed buyers who are willing to pay up into the close; this pressure diffuses into prices slowly because of limited arbitrage and inventory constraints. The premium is compensation for bearing the risk that this accumulation reverses, and it is distinct from a single-day reversal signal because the mechanism is persistence of positioning, not mean reversion of a gap.

Stocks whose volatility is concentrated intraday attract short-horizon liquidity providers who demand compensation for inventory risk; stocks whose volatility is concentrated overnight reflect information arriving when liquidity is thin.
