Persistent buying pressure into the close reflects informed accumulation that has not yet been fully impounded in price; retail/flow-driven liquidity provision is undercompensated, so stocks with consistently high close location tend to drift up over the following weeks. Unlike a single-day reversal signal, averaging over a window captures the sustained institutional footprint rather than noise.

## overnight_gap_absorption

**hypothesis**: Stocks with a large positive overnight_return but a weak intraday_return on the same day (gap up that fades) underperform over the next 5 days; stocks with a negative overnight gap that is bought intraday outperform, dollar-neutral.

**economic rationale**: Overnight returns are driven by news and off-hours order flow that may overreact because liquidity is thin. If a stock gaps up overnight but the intraday session fails to hold the move (intraday_return negative relative to the gap), the initial news is being absorbed/sold into, and the gap tends to partially reverse. Conversely a negative overnight gap that is bought back intraday signals informed support. The mechanism is underreaction to the intraday correction of an over-extrapolated overnight move.

## intraday_momentum_vs_overnight_noise

**hypothesis**: Stocks with high cumulative intraday_return over the trailing 20 days outperform stocks with low cumulative intraday_return over the next 5 days, dollar-neutral, and this signal is stronger than total-return momentum.

**economic rationale**: Intraday returns reflect continuous-session trading where informed traders and market makers interact under competitive conditions, whereas overnight returns are dominated by news jumps and thin off-hours flow. Cumulative intraday return over a window should therefore be a cleaner momentum signal than total return, which is contaminated by overnight noise. Stocks with strong sustained intraday accumulation should continue to outperform as information diffuses gradually.

- **decision**: abandon
- **reasoning**: The 20-day cumulative intraday-return signal has essentially zero cross-sectional IC (rank_ic 0.0003, IC-IR 0.005) and the grid's best Sharpe of 0.60 comes only from a sign() transform with decay 60, i.e. a near-binary bet on a signal whose continuous information content is nil. Critically, the edge flips sign when micro caps are removed (subuniverse Sharpe -0.11, subuniverse_flips_sign) and the variant PnL decorrelates (min_pnl_corr -0.28), meaning the headline result is concentrated in small illiquid names and not identified by the mechanism. Only 12.5% of configurations are positive and the holdout IC is negative (-0.0048), so the hypothesis that intraday accumulation carries cleaner momentum is contradicted.
- **diagnosis**: Signal is absent (near-zero IC); the apparent Sharpe comes from a sign transform on a noisy factor, concentrated in micro caps and unstable across variants, not from genuine intraday momentum.
