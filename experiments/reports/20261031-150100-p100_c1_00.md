## overnight_intraday_reversal_spread

**hypothesis**: Stocks whose cumulative overnight return over the past 20 days has strongly outpaced their cumulative intraday return will underperform over the next 5 days, and vice versa; i.e. the cross-sectional rank of (overnight minus intraday cumulative return) negatively predicts 5-day forward returns.

**economic rationale**: Overnight and intraday returns are driven by different clienteles: overnight moves are dominated by retail/after-hours order flow and news reactions that are often overextended, while intraday moves reflect institutional liquidity provision. When a stock's recent overnight returns have persistently exceeded its intraday returns, the overnight component is likely stretched and will mean-revert as informed intraday traders fade it. The premium is compensation for absorbing the temporary price pressure created by uninformed overnight demand.
