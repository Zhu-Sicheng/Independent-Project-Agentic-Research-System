## overnight_return_volatility_ratio

**hypothesis**: Stocks with a high 20-day ratio of mean absolute overnight return to total daily return volatility outperform low-ratio stocks over the next 5 trading days.

**economic rationale**: Overnight price moves reflect information absorption when liquidity is thin and participation is dominated by informed or institutional flow, whereas intraday moves embed retail noise and market-maker inventory effects. Stocks whose overnight returns are large relative to their total return volatility are those where informed accumulation is persistent; this information diffuses slowly into prices because arbitrage capital is reluctant to trade at the open. The premium is compensation for bearing the risk that this slow diffusion reverses or accelerates unexpectedly.
