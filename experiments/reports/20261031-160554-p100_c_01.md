## overnight_gap_absorption_ratio

**economic rationale**: Persistent overnight gaps that fail to be confirmed by intraday continuation reflect inventory pressure from overnight liquidity providers who must be compensated for absorbing news-driven order flow. When the overnight move is large relative to the day's realized range but the intraday session partially retraces it, the residual gap is a temporary pricing error driven by constrained risk-bearing, not information. Stocks with unabsorbed gaps should mean-revert as liquidity providers unwind hedges over the following week.
