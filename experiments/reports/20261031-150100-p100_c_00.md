## overnight_intraday_return_divergence

**hypothesis**: Over a 20-day formation window, stocks with high cumulative overnight return relative to cumulative intraday return outperform stocks with low relative overnight return over the next 5 trading days.

**economic rationale**: Overnight and intraday returns are driven by different clienteles: overnight moves reflect informed/after-hours order flow and news absorption, while intraday moves are dominated by liquidity provision and retail noise trading. Stocks whose recent returns are earned predominantly overnight (positive overnight, flat/negative intraday) attract persistent informed accumulation, whereas stocks whose gains come from intraday noise tend to reverse. The divergence between the two components is a cleaner signal than total return because it isolates the informed-flow channel from the noise channel.

best config: decay=0, transform=rank, cap_filter=0.3, windows=[5, 20], params={}

- **decision**: abandon
- **reasoning**: The mechanism is coherent but the evidence does not support it. Full-universe Sharpe is only 0.478 with rank_ic 0.0082, and it decays monotonically across segments: IS 0.578, OOS 0.375, holdout -0.355 (2026 alone at -0.79). The stability battery flags window_span_crosses_zero (span -0.13 to 0.84) and the decay span also dips negative (-0.13), so the result hinges on one arbitrary window/smoothing choice rather than a robust divergence effect.
- **diagnosis**: Signal is weak and non-stationary: the overnight-minus-intraday divergence edge existed modestly pre-2021, faded in OOS, and reversed in the 2025-2026 holdout. ## intraday_range_position_reversal

**hypothesis**: Stocks with a high average close_position over the trailing 20 days underperform stocks with a low average close_position over the next 5 trading days.

**economic rationale**: Where the close sits within the day's high-low range is a proxy for end-of-day order-flow imbalance. Stocks closing at the very top of their range have absorbed aggressive buying that is often liquidity-driven and mean-reverts, while stocks closing near the low have been sold by impatient sellers. Averaging this close_position over a short window captures persistent one-sided pressure that should reverse as liquidity providers are compensated for absorbing it.
